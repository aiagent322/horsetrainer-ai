Background

Craig Cameron

Craig Cameron was born in 1949 in Texas. He grew up on a ranch and competed in rodeo, riding bulls at the PRCA level. He lives in Bluff Dale, Texas. His wife is Dalene Cameron. His son Cole Cameron works with him training horses. Cameron is a clinician who has been on the road more than 44 weeks a year covering approximately 80,000 miles demonstrating horsemanship. He and Dalene are the creators and founders of Extreme Cowboy Racing and the Guts and Glory 100 Mile Ride for Special Ops. The Extreme Cowboy Association was formed in 2008.

Career

Achievements

Cameron has been the host of the award-winning series Ride Smart on RFD-TV for over a decade. He is the author of two bestselling Western Horseman books. He has been awarded two Silver Telly Awards. He was inducted into the Culver Military Academy Hall of Fame in 2009, the Texas Heroes Hall of Honor in 2012, and the Texas Cowboy Hall of Fame.

In 2010 Cameron won the Road to the Horse World Championship at the Tennessee Miller Coliseum in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, defeating Richard Winters and Ken McNabb. He was 61 years old. He said at the time: "Every time I've competed at Road to the Horse, I've left a piece of my heart in the arena. Now I'm getting a little bit of that back. I truly believe winning Road to the Horse is going to change my life. I am 61 years old and now I am a world champion and no-one can ever take that away from me. This may be the best day of my life." He later returned to Road to the Horse in 2016 as Horseman's Host alongside host Matt West.
