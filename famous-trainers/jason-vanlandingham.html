Overview

One of the Most Accomplished Reiners of His Generation

Jason Vanlandingham is one of the leading figures in modern reining competition and has become widely recognized as one of the National Reining Horse Association's most accomplished riders of his generation. Known for his consistency, competitive drive, and ability to develop elite futurity and derby horses, Vanlandingham built a career that placed him among the sport's top earners and most respected trainers. His accomplishments in major NRHA events, including historic success at The Run For A Million, helped establish him as one of the dominant riders in contemporary reining.

Raised in Texas, Vanlandingham grew up around horses and developed a strong interest in western riding and competition from an early age. Early in his career he worked his way through assistant training positions and gradually developed the skills required to compete at the highest levels of NRHA competition. His first NRHA association earnings came at the 2000 NRHA Derby, marking the start of one of the most successful careers in the sport.

2013 NRHA Futurity

Not Ruf At All — The Horse That Launched a Historic Career

2013 NRHA Open Futurity Champion
Not Ruf At All
Owned by Vaughn Zimmerman, Not Ruf At All gave Vanlandingham his breakout NRHA Open Futurity victory in 2013 — launching both horse and rider into the upper ranks of the reining industry. Together they went on to complete one of reining's rare Triple Crown achievements, winning the NRHA Futurity, NRHA Derby, and National Reining Breeders Classic.

Vanlandingham first completed the Triple Crown with Not Ruf At All, then repeated the achievement with another son of A Sparkling Vintage — making him the only rider in NRHA history to accomplish the Triple Crown twice.
Run For A Million

Back-to-Back Champion — A First in the Event's History

In recent years, Vanlandingham gained additional national attention through his performances at The Run For A Million, one of the sport's richest and most visible events. He became the first rider in history to win The Run For A Million Million Dollar Competition in back-to-back years, helping elevate his profile even further within and beyond the reining industry.
