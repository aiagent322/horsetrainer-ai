Background

Sandy Collier

Sandy Collier was born in Massachusetts and raised in New England. She competed in her first horse show at age six in an English saddle and was a competitive jumper by age 12. She was also a junior state champion in golf and excelled at tennis. She decided against college, traveled across Canada, and in 1972 arrived in Santa Barbara, California. At age 19 she found work at the Tajiguas Ranch in Santa Barbara, where her duties included barn chores, shoeing horses, colt-starting, saddle making, and rawhide braiding. With the help of Doug Ingersoll she began competing in the California Reined Cow Horse Association showing Quarter Horses. In 1979 she moved to Santa Ynez and worked for trainer Tom Shelly for one year before going out on her own. She started her own training business in 1980. She resides in Buellton, California.

She has said of her early years competing against men: "Those guys were just really good, and they were riding good horses. I simply had to stay home and work doubly hard until I could come and be competitive with them."

Career

Competitive Record

Collier first attended the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity in 1976 as a student. In 1991 she won the herd work preliminaries at the Snaffle Bit Futurity on Skip To My Joye. In 1992 her customers David and Paula Hunsicker sent her to find a Snaffle Bit Futurity prospect. She found Miss Rey Dry (Dry Doc × Starlita Seguin × Rey Jay) at the Tejon Ranch and purchased her. In 1993 Collier won the NRCHA World Championship Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Championship on Miss Rey Dry — becoming the first woman to win the event. She was the 1996 NRCHA World Champion Open Bridle. In 2001 she was NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Reserve Champion. In 2002 she won the AQHA World Champion Junior Working Cow Horse title on Sheza Shinette (owned by Holy Cow Performance Horses). In 2008 she won the NRCHA Hackamore Classic Championship.

She was inducted into the NRCHA Hall of Fame in 2013 and the National Cowgirl Hall of Fame in 2011. Horse & Rider named her one of the "Top 50 Riders of All Time in All Disciplines" in 2011. She received the Monty Roberts Lifetime Achievement Award in 2008. She is a AAA-rated NRCHA judge, has judged all major NRCHA events and the AQHA World Show multiple times, and has judged the World's Greatest Horseman and Road to the Horse. She is a member of the NRCHA Board of Directors.
