Early Life

Bergamo, Italy — A Dairy Farm, a Pony, and a Dream Nobody Around Him Understood

Andrea Fappani was born and raised in Bergamo, in the Lombardy region of northern Italy, the son of Sergio and Maddalisa Fappani — fourth-generation dairy farmers. Everything in his world pointed toward the farm, toward the cows, toward a life in Bergamo. But horses had a different plan.

Fappani's father introduced him to horses as a young child, and they began riding together. At the time, western performance equine events were almost non-existent in Europe. The riding world around Fappani was English — jumping, dressage, the disciplines that dominate the continent. He took to jumping quickly, spending his first two or three years in an English saddle. Then a friend showed him something different.

He discovered reining as a child. He was nine years old when he first saw it, and the precision, athleticism, and controlled power of the sliding stop hit him in a way that nothing else had. He switched disciplines immediately and never looked back. Western performance events barely existed in Europe at that point. He was building toward something he could not yet name in a country where nobody around him had any idea what it was.

Beyond the Arena

Teacher, Clinician, Television, and the Fappani Saddle

Andrea Fappani's influence extends well beyond the show pen. He is a respected clinician and teacher, sharing the philosophy that has driven his career — that champions are made in preparation, not performance; that excellence is daily, not occasional. He hosts the Along For The Ride with Andrea Fappani podcast, carrying his competitive philosophy and career wisdom to a global audience.

He has appeared on the hit television series Yellowstone and The Last Cowboy — bringing the world of elite reining to a mainstream audience in a way that few professionals in the discipline have achieved. His presence on those platforms has introduced the sport to millions of viewers who otherwise would never have encountered a sliding stop.

His collaboration with Bob's Custom Saddles produced the "Fappani Saddle" — designed from decades of real-world competitive experience and built around his uncompromising standards for balance, close contact, rider position, and freedom of movement. It is now the #1 highest-selling saddle model Bob's Custom Saddles produces, trusted by professionals and non-pros across western performance disciplines worldwide.
