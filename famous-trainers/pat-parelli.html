Background

Pat Parelli

Pat Parelli was born on March 17, 1954, in Livermore, California, into a family with no horse background. As a child he worked as a stablehand for nearby horse facilities. At age 13, horseman and trapper Freddie Ferrera of Livermore recognized his talent with horses and took him under his wing for the summers, teaching him about horses, dogs, cattle, and nature. He competed in rodeo during high school and graduated from Fresno State University with a degree in agricultural education. He was named the PRCA Bareback Rookie of the Year in 1972. He later trained mules and in the late 1970s and early 1980s rode a mule named Thumper at the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity, placing third. He credits mentor Troy Henry — one of the greatest western horsemen in the US — who taught him to be a horseman for five years; after Henry's death Parelli dedicated himself to spreading Henry's philosophy. He also followed the teachings of Ray Hunt, Tom Dorrance, and Ronnie Willis.

His first wife was Karen; they have a son, Caton (born 1984), and a daughter. Karen co-authored his first book and helped form the initial program. His second wife was Linda Parelli (née Paterson; born 1958 in Singapore, raised in Australia); she met Parelli in Australia in 1989, came to America in 1992, and they married in 1995; they divorced in 2020. Parelli has been headquartered in Pagosa Springs, Colorado since 1996.

Career

Parelli Natural Horsemanship

On March 1, 1982, Parelli gave his first seminar. In 1984 he began teaching clinics. In 1985 Parelli Natural Horsemanship became an official business. In 1991 he coined the term "Natural Horsemanship." The program has since spread to 76 countries and impacted over a million people. It is structured in four levels: Level 1: Your Journey Begins, Level 2: Winning the Seven Games, Level 3: Harmony & Creativity, Level 4: Refinement & Progression. He has two instructional centers — Pagosa Springs, Colorado and Ocala, Florida.
