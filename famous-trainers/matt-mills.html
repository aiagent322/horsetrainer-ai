Background

Matt Mills

Matt Mills grew up in Southern California. His mother had horses as a child and took him for riding lessons at age eight. He was introduced to horses by a Shetland pony named Dizzy at the Lakewood Equestrian Center in Long Beach, California. He rode the hour-long bus to attend lessons. He showed in almost every discipline except reining until he was about 18. The day after high school graduation in 1997 he went to Arizona for what was planned as a summer internship with NRHA Million Dollar Rider Dell Hendricks — and stayed for five years. He has said: "First week I was there we went to the NRHA Derby and he ties to win it with Randy Paul with a horse named Hangten Peppy, so my eyes were wide open." He joined NRHA as a youth member in 1997 and became an NRHA Professional in 2003. He has said of reining: "I picked reining because I LOVE speed combined with finesse. I ended up training when I found someone that would actually pay me to do it." He and his wife Karen have children Ryan, Alec, and Emma. He serves as premier horse trainer for Bosque Ranch in North Texas — the filming location for Yellowstone and 1883.

Career

Competitive Record

His first significant win came at the 2000 NRHA Futurity — Open L3 Reserve title with Dunnits Delight. His most notable accomplishment is winning the 2006 USET Open Reining Championship on Easy Otie Whiz (Topsail Whiz × Miss Poco Easy) in Lexington, Kentucky, with a career-high score of 231.5 — qualifying him for Team USA, which won gold at the 2006 World Equestrian Games in Aachen, Germany. He has said: "Easy Otie Whiz really improved the trajectory of my career. I made a lot of open level 4 finals on him and also attained my all-time highest score under five judges." He became an NRHA Million Dollar Rider in October 2020. He is consistently ranked on the NRHA Top Twenty Professionals Riders list. His top career earner is Wimpys Cool Breeze ($129,529). Other titles include NRHA Intermediate Open Futurity Reserve Champion, multiple National Reining Breeders Classic titles, RMRHA Summer Slide, High Roller Reining Classic, SWRHA Futurity, and NRHA Cowtown Classic. He has also appeared on the television series The Last Cowboy and Yellowstone.
