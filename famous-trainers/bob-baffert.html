Background

Bob Baffert

Robert A. Baffert was born on January 13, 1953, in Nogales, Arizona, the middle of seven children. His family raised cattle and chickens on a ranch in Nogales. His father bought Quarter Horses when Bob was ten, and he practiced racing them on a dirt track at the ranch. In his teens he worked as a jockey in informal Quarter Horse races near Nogales for $100 a day. At age 17 in 1970 he won his first official race at a legal track. He graduated from the University of Arizona's Race Track Industry Program with a Bachelor of Science degree. After college he began training Quarter Horses at a farm in Prescott, Arizona. In the 1980s he moved to California to work at Los Alamitos Race Course. In 1991 he switched to Thoroughbreds full-time. He married Jill Baffert in 2002. He was inducted into the National Museum of Racing and Hall of Fame in 2009.

Career

Racing Record

Baffert won the Eclipse Award for Outstanding Trainer in 1997, 1998, and 1999. He trained American Pharoah to win the 2015 Triple Crown — the first Triple Crown winner in 37 years — and Justify to win the 2018 Triple Crown. He is tied for the most Kentucky Derby wins with six: Silver Charm (1997), Real Quiet (1998), War Emblem (2002), American Pharoah (2015), Justify (2018), and Authentic (2020). Medina Spirit won the 2021 Derby but was later stripped of the title after testing positive for a banned substance. He holds the record for Preakness Stakes wins with seven. He was the first trainer in history to win the Kentucky Derby and Preakness in back-to-back years — with Silver Charm in 1997 and Real Quiet in 1998.

Other major wins include Point Given in the 2001 Preakness and Belmont Stakes, and War Emblem in the 2002 Kentucky Derby and Preakness. His first Thoroughbred purchase was Thirty Slews in 1988; Thirty Slews won the 1992 Breeders' Cup Sprint. In 2022 Baffert was banned from the Kentucky Derby for three years following the Medina Spirit drug testing violation. He returned to the race in 2025 with Citizen Bull, who finished 15th.
