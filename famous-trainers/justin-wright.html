Background

Justin Wright

Justin Wright was born and raised around cow horses in California. He had a successful youth and non-pro career and knew in his mid-teens that he wanted to become a horse trainer. He became an open rider in 2009. During the 2018 show season he accumulated $307,793 in earnings, and in 2019 at the NRCHA Celebration of Champions achieved NRCHA Million Dollar status. He has said: "It was a relief [to achieve a million dollars] because that was one of the goals and dreams I had; something I always wanted. But it was quite humbling once I got there. I looked up to every one of the Million Dollar trainers." He credits his customers and family with his success.

Career

Competitive Record

Since becoming an open rider in 2009, Wright has earned multiple NRCHA major limited-age event championship titles. He is based in Santa Maria, California. He competed as part of Team Red with Lloyd Cox and Shawn Flarida at The American Performance Horseman.
