Background

Todd Crawford

Todd Crawford had a lifelong passion for horses that led him to the competitive arena as a youth. He attended Findlay College (University of Findlay) in Ohio and was in the first class of students to graduate with Bachelor of Science degrees from the school established by legendary horseman Dale Wilkinson. He returned to Findlay as an instructor, then set up his own training facility in California. He eventually moved to Blanchard, Oklahoma, where he and his wife Pam operate Crawford Performance Horses and raised their daughters Carter and MacKinnon. He has said of the reined cow horse discipline: "To this day, I still feel like I have a lot of weaknesses in the cow horse. Just to me, my personal feeling is that I feel like there is a ton of stuff that I need to learn and I need to do better."

Career

Competitive Record

Crawford and his students have won more than three dozen World Championship titles in three major performance horse associations. He became an NRCHA Million Dollar Rider in 2006, the same year he won the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity. He is an NRCHA $3 Million Dollar Rider. His NRHA earnings exceed $900,000. Key horse: Hickorys Holly Cee, who made the Open finals in both the NRHA Futurity and the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity. He has won the AQHA Open World Championship, the AQHA Senior Reining World Championship, the USET Championship Gold Medal, the USEF Open Reining Championship, and the FEI World Reining Masters Finals Gold Medal. He was named Professional Horseman of the Year.
