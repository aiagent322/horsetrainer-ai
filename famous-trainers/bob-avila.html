Background

Bob Avila

Robert Charles "Bob" Avila was born in November 1951 in Redwood City, California. His father, Don Avila, was a rodeo cowboy and horse trainer. His mother, Pat Avila, worked at a western store and gave riding lessons. Avila showed his first horse at age five. He has said of the horsemen he grew up watching: "They were at all the shows, too, and literally helped raise me. They were true horsemen. I learned things from each one of them starting at a very young age that otherwise would have taken a lifetime to learn." Among his early influences he named Don Dodge, Tony Amaral Sr., Harry Rose Sr., Clyde Kennedy, and Jimmy Williams. At age 20 he went to work for NRCHA Hall of Fame member Tony Amaral. Todd Bergen worked for Avila for almost six years as a young trainer.

Avila operated Avila Training Stables in Temecula, California with his wife Dana. His son is BJ Avila. He died on November 9, 2024, at age 72, while attending the AQHA World Championship show in Oklahoma City. The season 5 finale of Yellowstone was dedicated to him.

Bob Avila said: "Horses have given me everything I have. Quarter Horses have helped make me what I am. They have been my life's work and I owe them everything."

Career

Competitive Record

Avila accumulated over 37 World Champion or Reserve World Champion titles in AQHA events ranging from performance to halter. He was the first recipient of the AQHA Professional Horseman award. He was inducted into the NRCHA Hall of Fame in 2006 and was an NRCHA Million Dollar Rider.

He won the Snaffle Bit Futurity Championship three times. He won the World's Greatest Horseman title twice — on Paid By Chic in 2000 and Light N Fine in 2007. He won the Magnificent 7 Reined Cow Horse competition three times. He is one of only two people to win both the $100,000 NRCHA Open Futurity and the $100,000 NRHA Open Futurity. He won the NRHA Futurity Open Championship in 1994 on Lenas Wright On (Smart Little Lena × Slide Me Again).

Key horses trained include Major Bonanza, Major Investment, The Major Leaguer, Mist N Smoke, Smoke Em Okie, Wright On, Smart Lil Calboy, Smart Zanolena, and Chics Magic Potion. He also produced the magazine Ride With Bob Avila. He appeared as himself in Episode 8 of Season 3 of Yellowstone.
