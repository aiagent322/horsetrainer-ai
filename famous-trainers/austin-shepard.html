Background

Austin Shepard

Austin Shepard grew up around cutting horses. His late father, Sam Shepard, was an NCHA Hall of Fame rider and trainer and an Equi-Stat Elite $2 Million Rider. Sam also received the NCHA Zane Schulte Award in 2009. Austin's parents split when he was young; he and his mother moved into the city and he would go ride with his dad in the summers. He has said that not being around the horses all the time only increased his interest in riding. The only job he ever had in cutting was with his father. He grew up around trainers including Bill Riddle, Ronnie Rice, Jody Galyean, Buster Welch, and Pat Earnheart. Other influences include Paul Hansma.

Austin Shepard has been an active NCHA member since 1985. He operates Austin Shepard Cutting Horses at the Circle S Ranch in Summerdale, Alabama. His wife Stacy was inducted into the NCHA Non-Pro Riders Hall of Fame in 2009 and has earned more than $500,000 in cutting competition. Their son Cade is the youngest person ever inducted into the NCHA Non-Pro Hall of Fame. Daughter Caylee also competes in cutting. Four members of the Shepard family are NCHA Hall of Fame inductees.

Austin Shepard on cutting: "It's very cow related. I'm not very good at mechanical training, it's just not the way I was taught."

On Buster Welch: "Anybody that has [ridden] a cutting horse has been influenced by him whether they know it or not."

Career

Competitive Record

Shepard was inducted into the NCHA Riders Hall of Fame in 2005. He has accumulated over $11 million in NCHA lifetime earnings, currently second in the all-time NCHA standings. He has ridden 17 horses to more than $100,000 in earnings.

He won the 2007 NCHA Futurity Open Championship aboard High Brow CD (High Brow Cat × Sweet Little CD × CD Olena), a 2004 stallion bred by Wendel Ranches Inc. of Bastrop, Texas. High Brow CD earned $543,161 in his career.

In 2017 he won the NCHA Open World Championship with Deluxe Checks, the NCHA Summer Spectacular Open Championship, and the NCHA Futurity Open Championship with Dual Reyish (Hottish × A Little Reylena × Dual Rey) — his second Futurity title. Dual Reyish was from Hottish's first crop of foals. The owner of Dual Reyish, Billy Wolf, only met both horse and trainer the night before the final. In 2018 he won the Breeder's Invitational Derby Open Championship.

He also rode Thomas E Hughes (2009), Bet Hesa Cat (2011), and Bamacat (2015) to World Champion Stallion titles. Sir Long Legs was the 2016 NCHA Futurity Open Reserve Champion.

Top career horses by earnings: High Brow CD ($543,161), San Tule Uno ($410,266), Dual Reyish ($383,548), Bamacat ($351,522), Sir Long Legs ($337,104).

Austin Shepard received the 2022 NCHA Zane Schulte Award, presented to a professional trainer who exemplifies integrity, service, values, and respect of peers.
