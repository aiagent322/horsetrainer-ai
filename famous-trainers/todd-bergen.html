Background

Todd Bergen

Todd Bergen is based in Eagle Point, Oregon. He is not from a horse family. He grew up showing in 4H and breed shows. He has said: "I started showing in 4H as a kid and then went to breed shows here, you had to learn how to show all types of horses. I'm not from a horse family so I learned from a lot of guys. It wasn't until I spent time as an assistant trainer to Bob Avila that I learned about training reining and cow horses." He worked for Bob Avila for almost six years. While still an assistant trainer to Avila, Bergen won the 1995 NRHA Futurity — his most memorable moment by his own account. He later went out on his own in 1990, initially focusing on reining horses. Andrea Fappani worked under Bergen for four years as a young trainer before going out on his own.

Career

Competitive Record

Bergen won the NRHA Open Futurity twice: in 1995 on Todasmyluckyday (while still assistant trainer to Bob Avila) and in 1998 on Chic Please (Smart Chic Olena × San O Lenita, owned by Arcese Quarter Horses). Chic Please was also 1998 NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Reserve Champion. He won the 1995 NRHA Derby Open Championship on Lean With Me and the 1996 NRHA Derby Open Championship on Jerry Lees Surprise (Surprise Enterprise × Mizzen Topsail, owned by Douglas Holmes). He won the 2001 NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity on Boonlight Dancer and the 2011 NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity on Shiners Dun Juan. He won the 2002 and 2006 NRCHA Derby Championships. He was the 2012 Equistat No. 1 Leading Rider of Reining, Working Cow & Cutting Horses. He won the 2014 NRCHA Open Stakes Championship on This One Time and the 2015 NRHA Derby on Platinum Vintage.

In 2004 Bergen became an NRHA Million Dollar Rider. He also became the sixth NRCHA Million Dollar Rider and was the first rider ever to achieve Million Dollar status in two different associations. He has accumulated over $2.2 million in NRHA earnings and over $2.5 million in NRCHA earnings. He has produced instructional DVDs: Framework of Performance and Fundamentals of Cow Work.

Philosophy

  • "Nerves come from uncertainty" Preparation is the antidote — Bergen's mental philosophy, practiced across 30+ years of Futurity competition
  • "I've always tried to just worry about myself" Watch only people you know and respect; don't be unduly influenced by the crowd or the competition
  • "Consistency is what makes a solid horse" Training philosophy — no shortcuts, no gimmicks
  • "To this day I feel like I have weaknesses" Lifelong commitment to learning, regardless of career achievement
