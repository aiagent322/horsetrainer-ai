Background

Matt Gaines

Matt Gaines grew up around horses. His father, Dick Gaines, was also a cutting horse trainer and his biggest influence. Despite his father's career, Matt's parents urged him to go to college rather than become a professional trainer. He earned an agriculture business degree from Tarleton State University and went to work for tack manufacturer Dennis Moreland. He later decided to make cutting his profession and went to work for the late Charles Spence. He never worked for any trainers other than his father and then Spence, though he has trained alongside professionals including Winston Hansma and Paul Hansma. He was a highly successful non-pro competitor before turning professional. He and his wife Tara live in Weatherford, Texas, where they operate Matt Gaines Cutting Horses. He joined J Five Horse Ranch as head trainer in January 2023.

On training: "Consistency is the key when it comes to your position on a cow and trying to teach the horse the same thing every day until they get it."

Career

Competitive Record

Gaines has over $10 million in NCHA lifetime earnings and is the No. 3 NCHA All-Time Leading Open Rider. His first Open win came in 2001 at the Will Rogers Coliseum on Sunettes Dually, marking a 231 in the Super Stakes Derby. He won the 2016 NCHA Open Futurity Championship on Second Spot — a mare out of Reycy Moon whom he had also trained. He is one of only three riders to have shown two NCHA Horses of the Year: Little Pepto Gal and Smooth As A Cat. He won three consecutive Mercuria Championships (four total), including a 234 score at the El Rancho Futurity — tied for the highest score ever marked in NCHA history. He was the 2023 NCHA Futurity Open Reserve Champion on Sugarr Daddy. He also won the 2021 NCHA World Finals Open Championship. Key horses in his career include Nu I Wood, One Time Pepto, Im Coutin Checks, Smooth As A Cat, Second Spot, and Sugarr Daddy.
