Background

Al Dunning

Al Dunning was born in 1950 in Chicago, Illinois. His family moved to Arizona when he was eight years old. At age 12 he picked oranges for a restaurant in Scottsdale. He went on to further his horsemanship with mentors including Jim Paul, John Hoyt, and Don Dodge. In the early 1980s he worked with Hall of Fame trainer Don Dodge to develop his cutting horse skills.

He has owned and operated Almosta Ranch in Scottsdale, Arizona since 1970. He married his wife Becky in 1971. They have two children, daughter McKenzie and son Grady. Becky is a well-known horse show announcer and co-founded America's Horse Cares, a division of the AQHA Foundation focused on therapeutic riding charities.

Expensive Hobby

Expensive Hobby

Expensive Hobby was born in 1971, sired by Hobby Horse and out of Jan's Helen by AQHA Champion Stormy's Sugar. Mehl Lawson started the buckskin gelding in hackamore classes. Al Dunning trained and showed Expensive Hobby for most of the gelding's career. According to a book on reining co-written by Dunning and Pat Close, Hobby won 29 of 32 hackamore classes and finished first in more than 100 reining or cow horse events. He won the AQHA World Championship in working cow horse in 1976, and AQHA World titles in senior reining and senior working cow horse in 1979. He won the NRHA Open Championship at the 1980 All American Quarter Horse Congress. He was a four-time AQHA Champion. Expensive Hobby died in 1996 and was inducted into the AQHA Hall of Fame in 2007. He is also a Breyer model.

Al Dunning has said: "Other horses have been just as talented – maybe even better – but you've never seen one with so much strength. He would just pile-drive his back end into the dirt."

Career

Achievements and Recognition

Al Dunning and his students have earned 57 World and Reserve World titles across NCHA, AQHA, NRCHA, and NRHA. His most memorable personal win was the AQHA World Championship in senior cutting on More Oats Please in 1988 — a horse whose dam he owned and whom he raised and trained himself. He is an EquiStat Elite Million Dollar Winner and an NCHA Million Dollar Rider.

Awards include: AQHA Professional Horseman of the Year (1996), NCHA Zane Schulte Trainer of the Year (2003), Monte Roberts Equitarian Award (2004), Western Horseman Award (2017), 2021 PCCHA Smith Sportsmanship recipient. He was inducted into the AZQHA Hall of Fame in 2016.

He has authored five books: Reining, Reining Revised, The Art of Hackamore Training, The Ultimate Level of Horsemanship, and Down The Fence. He has produced six instructional videos. He operates Team AD International (online training), AD Tack (online store), and Dunning Consulting LLC.

He has served as a past president of the AZQHA and the Arizona Cutting Horse Association, as director at large of the AQHA, and as chairman of the AQHA Judges Committee and Show Committee. He holds an NRCHA judge's card and has judged the NRHA Futurity four times, AQHA World Show three times, and NRCHA Derby twice. He is one of the founding members of the Arizona Quarter Horse Youth Association and was its charter president.

He has mentored professional trainers including Brad Barkemeyer, Patrick Flaherty, Chris Johnsrud, Casey Hinton, Pete Kyle, John Pipkin, Brett Stone, Mike Wood, Jade Keller, and Cody Lamont.

Al Dunning has said: "I have been riding most of my life. A great family, loyal friends, and amazing horses have blessed me throughout. I wouldn't change a thing."
