Background

Joe Heim

Joe Heim was born in 1949. He lives in Thackerville, Oklahoma. His wife is Holly. He trains primarily Quarter Horses in cutting, reining, and AQHA performance horse disciplines. He is an AQHA World Champion and NRHA Champion, and is inducted into the NCHA Rider Hall of Fame.

Career

Competitive Record

Heim won the 1981 NCHA Futurity Open Championship on Colonel Lil (sired by Colonel Freckles). He then won the NCHA Triple Crown with Docs Okie Quixote — a 1980 AQHA stallion sired by Doc Quixote out of Jimmette Too by Johnny Tivio — winning the 1983 NCHA Futurity Open, the 1984 NCHA Super Stakes Open, and the 1984 NCHA Derby Open. Docs Okie Quixote was the first horse to win the NCHA Triple Crown owned, trained, and ridden by the same person. Docs Okie Quixote's Triple Crown earnings totaled $335,095 and his NCHA Lifetime Earnings totaled $599,109. Docs Okie Quixote died in 1985 after siring only one crop of foals. He is an NCHA Horse Hall of Fame inductee. Heim's total NCHA lifetime earnings as of 2012 were $1,382,487.
