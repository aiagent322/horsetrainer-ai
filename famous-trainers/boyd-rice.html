Background

Boyd Rice

Boyd Rice was born into a cutting horse family. His father, Sonny Rice, was a cutting horse trainer who won an NCHA World Championship on Jazzote. His mother is Gloria. His brother Matlock Rice and his uncles Raymond and Ronnie Rice are also cutting horse trainers. Boyd was about five years old when the family moved from Kansas to Brenham, Texas in 1970. He began showing in youth cutting contests at around age seven. He has said his father was really the only coach he had as a youngster. After marrying his wife Halee, he moved to Spearman, Texas in the Texas Panhandle, where he spent approximately 15 years mixing ranch work with starting colts and showing cutting horses. His son Tarin Rice won the 2012 NCHA Open Futurity Championship, becoming the youngest open trainer in history to win that event, and was later inducted into the NCHA Hall of Fame.

Career

Competitive Record

Rice rode Nesterote to his first major NCHA Open Finals at the 1995 Bonanza Cutting. His first major NCHA event championship came in 2001 on Can You Handle It at the Memphis Futurity. In 2002 he earned his first NCHA Open Futurity Finals slot on Meradas Rockalena. That same year he entered the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity for the first time on Deltas Color, finishing sixth in the Open and third in the Limited Open.

In 2005 he won the MillionHeir Cutting Open Championship on MH Bodees Starlena, the NCHA Open World Championship on Bobs Hickory Rio, and the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Reserve and Intermediate Open Championships on Playin Attraction — with single-year earnings exceeding $676,000. He won the 2007 NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Championship on Oh Cay N Short. In 2008 he won the NCHA Summer Spectacular Championship on Peptos Stylish Sue. In 2009 he won the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Reserve Championship on Picka Patcha Pepto. His lifetime earnings exceed $4 million in the Western performance horse industry.
