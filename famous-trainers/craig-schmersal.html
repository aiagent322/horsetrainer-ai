Background

Craig Schmersal

Craig Schmersal was born and raised in Columbus Grove, in northwest Ohio. His father Chuck inspired and supported his interest in horses. He has credited summers spent riding with multiple NRHA Futurity Champion Mike Flarida as an integral part of his development. He met his wife Ginger in Arizona in the early 1990s when both were working with the same Scottsdale trainer. They have four children and are based in Scottsdale, Arizona. He has said of horses: "I think the thing that I have learned most about horses is that I don't know a lot about horses." He has been active on the NRHA Judge's Committee and teaches the NRHA Judges School.

Career

Competitive Record

Schmersal has only missed the NRHA Futurity finals once since 1995. He is the No. 3 all-time NRHA earnings leader with over $5 million in career earnings. He is the only Level 4 Open champion of all five major events: The Run for a Million, the NRHA Futurity, the NRHA Derby, the National Reining Breeders Classic, and the All American Quarter Horse Congress. He was named 2019 NRHA Horseman of the Year.

Major wins include: 1994 NRHA Open and Limited Open World Championship on Cee Blair Sailor (owned by David Connor), also won the NRHA Open at the 1994 Americana in Germany on the same horse; 2002 FEI World Equestrian Games Team Gold on Tidal Wave Jack (reining's debut at the WEG, Jerez, Spain); 2004 NRHA Derby Open Championship on Commanders Nic; 2005 FEI World Reining Masters CRI-A Open Championship on Tidal Wave Jack; 2008 CRI-A Open USEF National Championship on Mr Dual Rey; 2010 FEI WEG Team Gold and Individual Silver on Mister Montana Nic; 2010 National Reining Breeders Classic Open Championship on Mister Montana Nic; 2011 FEI World Reining Finals and Ariat Tulsa Reining Classic CRI-A Championship on Miss Lil Addy Tude; 2014 AQHA Congress Futurity Championship on Xtra Electric Meter; 2017 NRHA Futurity Championship on Face The Attitude; 2019 Run for a Million Co-Championship (inaugural event).

He also promotes NRHA Million Dollar Sire Pale Face Dunnit (Gunner × Lena Dun It). He appeared on the CMT television series The Last Cowboy produced by Taylor Sheridan.
