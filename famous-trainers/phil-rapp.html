Background

Phil Rapp

Phil Rapp is based in Weatherford, Texas. He was introduced to the horse industry through his parents, Jerry and Nancy Rapp, who got into the cutting business. He began as a youth competitor, then competed as a Non-Pro, and eventually became a professional trainer. His wife Mary Ann Rapp is also a competitor; she was the 1997 Non-Pro World Champion on Tap O Lena. Their son Ryan Rapp also competes. Phil Rapp is inducted in both the NCHA Rider Hall of Fame (Open Division) and the NCHA Non-Pro Hall of Fame. He has served as NCHA Director and Executive Committee member, was elected NCHA vice-president in 2016, and became NCHA president in June 2018.

He has said to riders starting out: "Don't jump in with both feet. Take your time, get your bearings about you within the business. Find somebody that you trust that has a good reputation and begin a relationship with that person and listen to what they have to say."

Career

Competitive Record

Phil Rapp has accumulated an Equi-Stat record of $9,935,577 as a rider — the No. 2 NCHA All-Time Leading Open Money Earner as of that figure. Together with Mary Ann, he has owned the winners of more than $10.6 million and bred the winners of more than $9.1 million.

His defining horse was Tap O Lena (1990–2015), a bay Quarter Horse mare he bred, trained, and showed to 15 major NCHA aged event championships. Mary Ann also showed Tap O Lena, winning two Non-Pro championships including the 1997 Non-Pro World Championship. Tap O Lena became a respected producer of champion cutting horses.
