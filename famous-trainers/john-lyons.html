Background

John Lyons

John Lyons was born in 1947. He grew up in Arizona and played baseball at the University of Arizona. He was more interested in sports than horses as a young man. He was 24 when he discovered horses. He began training horses while working a cattle ranch on Divide Creek, near Silt, on Colorado's Western Slope. He is self-taught, developing his techniques from observation and experience rather than from any one trainer. His first horse was Ike, a Quarter Horse gelding he bought for $350 including a saddle and tack box. He has said of horses: "It's a disease, worse than probably any addiction." He lives and works with his family in Parachute, Colorado.

Career

Clinics, Publications, and Recognition

Lyons has been presenting training clinics and horsemanship symposia since 1980. He is known as "America's Most Trusted Horseman." He is the founder of John Lyons' Perfect Horse magazine. He has authored or co-authored multiple equine books including Lyons on Horses. He and his son Josh Lyons developed a trainer certification program with more than 300 professional certified trainers worldwide. His children Josh Lyons, Brandi Lyons, Sandy Lyons, and Tammy Lyons are also involved in horsemanship. He and Josh have competed in Road to the Horse; John competed shortly after knee replacement surgery. He has estimated that he has helped train approximately 7,000 horses in his career.

Awards include: Equitana USA's Modern Masters Award for Outstanding Horsemanship and the University of Louisville's John W. Galbreath Award for outstanding contributions to the horse industry. He has said: "I strive to find easier, more effective ways to communicate to both students and horses. My training methods make riding so much easier to accomplish, and we as riders begin to enjoy our riding much more because it is less complicated."
