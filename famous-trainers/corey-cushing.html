Background

From the Appaloosa World to the Top of Reined Cow Horse Competition

Corey Cushing is an American horse trainer and competitor best known for his accomplishments in the National Reined Cow Horse Association (NRCHA). Over the course of his career, Cushing became one of the leading riders in modern reined cow horse competition through multiple Snaffle Bit Futurity championships, World's Greatest Horseman titles, derby championships, and multi-million-dollar earnings.

Cushing grew up around horses and began riding at a young age. He first became involved in the Appaloosa horse world before transitioning into reining and reined cow horse competition. During his early development, he worked for several established horse trainers and spent time learning from prominent professionals including Noel Skinner, Todd Bergen, Teddy Robinson, Bob Avila, and other leading cow horse trainers.

His first major professional breakthrough came in 1999 at the National Reining Horse Association Futurity. At nineteen years old, while working as an assistant trainer, Cushing won the NRHA Intermediate Open Futurity Championship aboard Twice As Shiney and also qualified for the Open Finals. That success helped establish him as a rising competitor within the western performance horse industry.

Reined Cow Horse Career

CD Diamond, Four Futurity Titles, and Three World's Greatest Horseman Championships

Cushing established his own training operation at approximately age twenty-three and steadily built a reputation as one of the sport's top open riders. His first major NRCHA championship came in 2007 when he won the NRCHA Stallion Stakes Open Championship aboard CC Spin Cycle. Two years later, he won the 2009 NRCHA Derby Championship riding Smart Boons for owners Kevin and Sydney Knight.

One of the defining moments of Cushing's career occurred in 2012 when he rode CD Diamond to win the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Championship. During that same event, Cushing also became the youngest NRCHA Million Dollar Rider in association history at the time. The partnership between Cushing and CD Diamond became one of the most recognized horse-and-rider combinations in the sport. Their 2012 futurity victory earned more than $100,000, and the horse later became an influential sire within the cow horse industry.

Cushing added a second NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Championship in 2014 aboard Good Time, further strengthening his position among the sport's elite competitors. He continued adding major titles throughout the following decade, including additional futurity championships, derby victories, and three World's Greatest Horseman championships — in 2015, 2019, and 2022.
