Background

Corey Cushing

Corey Cushing grew up riding horses and started out in the Appaloosa world. He went to work for Noel Skinner. He worked for reining trainers before John Slack and Todd Bergen introduced him to working cow horses. Teddy Robinson taught him about going down the fence and working cows. Bob Avila and others in the cow horse industry also helped him along the way. He went out on his own at age 23. He has said: "I grew up riding horses my whole life. I worked for a lot of reining horse trainers and as time went on I started doing reined cow horses." He and his wife Kristen operate Corey Cushing Performance Horses in Scottsdale, Arizona. They have three children: Caleb, Lincoln, and Capri. He has served as NRCHA President.

Career

Competitive Record

At age 19 in 1999, Cushing won the NRHA Futurity Intermediate Open Championship and qualified for the Open Finals aboard Twice As Shiney (Shining Spark × Shine A Mite × Doc O Dynamite). In 2007 he won his first major NRHA title, the Stakes Open Championship, on CC Spin Cycle (Ray Gay Quixote × Lenas Peppy Cutter × Smart Peppy Lena), owned by Cathy Corrigan Frank. He won the 2009 NRCHA Derby on Smart Boons (Peptoboonsmal × Smart Little Easter × Smart Little Lena), owned by Kevin and Sydney Knight.

In 2012 he won the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Championship on CD Diamond (CD Olena × Shiners Diamond Girl × Shining Spark), owned by San Juan Ranch, and became the youngest NRCHA Million Dollar Rider at age 32. He said at the time: "It's dang sure something to be proud of. It's kind of like a Cinderella story, I guess. My hat's off to my customers and the great horses I've had the opportunity to show." He has won the NRCHA Snaffle Bit Futurity Open Championship three times and the NRCHA World's Greatest Horseman three times, including a win on Hott Rod. He has been a five-time AQHA working cow horse World Champion and has earned more than 2,500 AQHA open performance points. In 2025 he became the first NRCHA Four Million Dollar Rider — the all-time NRCHA earnings leader. His bucket list goal is the NRCHA Hackamore Classic, which he has said is the one major title he has not yet won. Son Caleb won the 2022 AQHYA World Championship in boxing aboard Corey's horse SJR Diamond Rey.
