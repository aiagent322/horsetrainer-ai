Background

Buster Welch

Fay Owen "Buster" Welch was born on May 23, 1928, in Sterling City, Texas. He died on June 12, 2022, in Abilene, Texas, at the age of 94. His wife was Sheila Dolin Welch. He was inducted into the NCHA Rider Hall of Fame, NCHA Member Hall of Fame, AQHA Hall of Fame, and Texas Cowboy Hall of Fame. He received the 2012 National Golden Spur Award for his outstanding contributions to the ranching and livestock industry. Austin Shepard said of him at his death: "Anybody that has [ridden] a cutting horse has been influenced by him whether they know it or not."

Career

Competitive Record

Welch won the NCHA World Championship four times. He won the NCHA World Championship on Marion's Girl in 1954 and 1956. In 1962 he and other competitors started the NCHA Futurity. The first NCHA Futurity was held at the Nolan County Coliseum in Sweetwater, Texas, and Welch rode Money's Glo in the event. He won the NCHA Futurity five times: Money's Glo (1962), Chickasha Glo (1963), Rey Jay's Pete (1966), Dry Doc (1971), and Peppy San Badger (1977). He also trained Mr San Peppy.

Key horses he trained and competed include Marion's Girl, Chickasha Mike, Money's Glo, Chickasha Glo, Rey Jay's Pete, Dry Doc, Mr San Peppy, and Peppy San Badger.
