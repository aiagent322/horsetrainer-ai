Background

A Texas Horseman — Building from the Ground Up

Casey Deary is an American reining horse trainer, competitor, and clinician best known for his accomplishments in National Reining Horse Association (NRHA) competition. He is based in Weatherford, Texas, where he operates Deary Performance Horses alongside his wife Nicole Deary.

Deary grew up in Texas and became involved with horses and western riding at a young age. He began developing horses and competing in reining while still a teenager. Early in his professional career, he trained horses for clients while steadily building his reputation within regional reining competition. His early successes included wins at Texas Reining Horse Association events and AQHA competition.

One of Deary's first significant accomplishments came in 2002 when he won the Limited Open division at the NRHA Futurity riding Hesa Turbo Chic. During the following years, he continued advancing in NRHA competition while developing young horses and expanding his client base. Horses such as Little Bay Starlight and Ciscos Roan Ranger helped elevate his profile nationally during the mid-2000s.

Championship Career

Three NRHA Futurity Titles and a $3M Milestone

Deary achieved his breakthrough at the highest level of the sport in 2012 when he won the NRHA Level 4 Open Futurity Championship riding Americasnextgunmodel. The mare later became one of the most influential horses associated with his career. In 2015, he earned his second NRHA Level 4 Open Futurity Championship aboard Shesouttayourleague. These victories established him among the top riders in the industry and helped him become an NRHA Million Dollar Rider.

Throughout the following decade, Deary continued compiling major championships at NRHA and National Reining Breeders Classic (NRBC) events. He won NRHA Derby Level 4 Open Championships aboard horses including Magnum Starlights, Shesouttayourleague, and Americasnexttopgun. In 2021, he earned his third NRHA Futurity Level 4 Open Championship riding Ten Thirty.

In 2022, Deary became an NRHA Three Million Dollar Rider following a victory at the NRHA Derby aboard Americasnexttopgun. Industry reports noted that only a small number of riders in NRHA history had surpassed the three-million-dollar mark at that time. In 2023, Deary and Down Right Amazing won major titles including the NRHA Derby Level 4 Open Championship and The American Performance Horseman reining division.

Deary Performance Horses

Training, Coaching, Breeding, and Online Education

Beyond competition, Deary became known as a clinician and mentor within the reining industry. Through Deary Performance Horses, he provides training, coaching, horse development, breeding services, and educational content for riders ranging from non-pro exhibitors to elite open competitors. His operation also includes online video training programs and stallion management.

Industry publications and interviews frequently describe Deary's horsemanship style as emphasizing consistency, horse confidence, and maintaining horses mentally and physically throughout long show careers. He has also publicly discussed the importance of faith and family in his career and personal life.
