Background

Casey Deary

Casey Deary was born in 1980 and grew up in Texas. His parents did not own horses when he was a child. By age 16 he was already buying low-cost unbroken horses, training them, and reselling them to build a training business. At 16 he was in a riding accident in Terrell, Texas — his horse spooked at a gate, he fell off, his foot caught in the stirrup, and the horse dragged him into a steel pipe fence. His friend Alan Chappell rushed him to the hospital. Unable to eat, he lost close to 60 pounds recovering. He and his wife Nicole started Deary Performance Horses in Weatherford, Texas in 2000. They have four children: Wyatt, Owen, Joy, and Olivia. Casey Deary said: "I feel like God put me here to ride these horses and use these opportunities to spread His word."

Career

Competitive Record

Deary began competing in NRHA in 1996. He won the 2012 NRHA Futurity Open Championship on Americasnextgunmodel and the 2015 NRHA Futurity Open Championship on Shesouttayourleague — winning a run-off and earning the $161,466 check that also made him the 24th NRHA Million Dollar Rider. He has won the NRHA Futurity four times total. He won the National Reining Breeders Classic in 2020 and the NRHA Derby in 2022 on Americasnexttopgun. He is ranked No. 4 in NRHA Leading Lifetime Riders with over $6 million in career earnings. He has won two AQHA World titles and one AQHA Reserve World Championship in reining. He is an AQHA Professional Horseman. He has qualified and competed in The Run For A Million every year since its inception in 2019. He won the 2022 Run For A Million Shootout Class on Down Right Amazing. He has won the American Performance Horseman reining title three times. He and his family have been featured in the CMT series The Last Cowboy, produced by Taylor Sheridan.
