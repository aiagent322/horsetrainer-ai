My horse does not want to ride away from the property with the barn what should I do?

A horse that is reluctant to leave the barn or property — that jiggs, calls to other horses, tries to turn back, plants his feet, or escalates in anxiety the further he travels from home — is exhibiting barn sourness or herd-boundness, one of the most common behavioral challenges in everyday riding.

My horse was gelded last year and acts a little studdy around a mare in season is there anything I can do?

Studdy behavior in a recently gelded horse is common, well understood, and in most cases resolves on its own with time — so the first thing to know is that what you are dealing with is normal and not a sign that something went wrong with the procedure or that you have a permanent management problem on your hands.
