How do I handle a horse that rears, and what causes rearing in the first place?

Rearing is one of the most dangerous behaviors a horse can develop under saddle, and it demands a thoughtful response rather than a reactive one. A rider who panics, grabs the reins, or leans backward when a horse rears is in serious danger of being flipped over on, which is one of the most catastrophic outcomes in horsemanship.
